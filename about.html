About Photo Compressor ExamReady

What is ExamReady?

Photo Compressor ExamReady is a free browser-based tool that helps students and applicants resize and compress photos and signatures for online exam application forms.

Many government and competitive exam portals (such as SSC, UPSC, Railway, Police, and state-level recruitment) require photos and signatures under specific file-size limits. Common requirements include passport photos under 50 KB and signatures under 20 KB, though exact limits vary by portal. ExamReady targets these common sizes by default.

Tools Available

  • Photo Compressor & Resizer: Resize passport-size photos to 350×450 px and signatures to 300×120 px, with JPEG compression targeting common exam-form file-size ranges.
  • Image to PDF: Combine multiple images into a single PDF with configurable page size, orientation, and margins.
  • PDF Merge: Combine multiple PDF files into one document.
  • PDF Split: Extract pages from a PDF into separate files by page range.
  • PDF Compress: Re-serialize a PDF with object streams and strip unnecessary metadata. Results vary depending on the input PDF; not all PDFs will see a size reduction.
  • PDF to Image: Render PDF pages to PNG or JPG images at selectable resolutions.

How It Works

Photo and PDF processing runs in your web browser using client-side JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip). Your photos and documents are not uploaded to any server for processing.

The website does load third-party resources for functionality: Google Fonts for typography, CDN-hosted JavaScript libraries, advertisement scripts, and an external QR code API for the UPI donation feature. These third-party services may set their own cookies or collect usage data according to their own privacy policies. See our Privacy Policy for details.
