Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Photo Compressor ExamReady. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

Photo Compressor - Exam Ready runs entirely inside your device's web browser using client-side technologies (Canvas / JavaScript). Your photos, signatures, and documents are NEVER uploaded to any external server, cloud storage, or third party.

2. Data Collection and Storage

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not store, view, or retain your uploaded files or compressed images.
  • No user accounts or logins are required to use this tool.

3. Local Storage

The application uses your browser's localStorage to save:

  • Usage counters and credit reset timestamps for the free-tier limit.
  • Pro/paid pass status and expiry dates if you purchase a plan.
  • UI preferences such as selected presets.

This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising

This website displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (including Adsterra). These networks may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads and collect anonymized usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control, and are not responsible for, data collected by these third-party services.

5. External Services

The website loads resources from external sources:

  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • CDN-hosted JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip) for PDF and image processing.
  • QR code API (api.qrserver.com) to generate UPI payment QR codes — this transmits only the UPI ID and amount, not your personal data.

These services may set their own cookies or collect usage data according to their respective privacy policies.
